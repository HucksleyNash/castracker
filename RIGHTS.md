# Rights and removal concerns

Castracker is a source-code project and does not host a media catalog. Users are
responsible for obtaining permission or another legal basis before downloading
or redistributing media.

If repository content—not a user's private local library—raises a copyright,
trademark, privacy, or other rights concern, open a GitHub issue titled
`Rights concern` and provide:

- the exact repository path or URL at issue;
- the work, mark, or information concerned;
- your relationship to the relevant right or person;
- the requested correction or removal; and
- a private contact method if follow-up is needed.

Do not post unnecessary personal data, credentials, or copies of the protected
work. Maintainers may remove or revise material while evaluating a good-faith
report. Nothing in this process limits the formal reporting mechanisms provided
by GitHub or applicable law.

Castracker is not affiliated with or endorsed by any podcast publisher, rights
holder, media platform, Audiobookshelf, Deno, FFmpeg, or yt-dlp.
